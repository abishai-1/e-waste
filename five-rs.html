The 5 R's Priority Ladder

Waste management begins long before reaching the bin. Follow this priority ladder to reduce household waste at source.

First Priority

1. Refuse (నిరాకరించండి)

Say no to unnecessary single-use items, unneeded packaging, and disposable plastic before they enter your home.

Practical household examples:
  • Politely decline single-use plastic carry bags when shopping; carry your own durable cloth bag.
  • Refuse single-use plastic cutlery and straws when ordering takeaway meals.
Second Priority

2. Reduce (తగ్గించండి)

Lower the total amount of waste generated by purchasing only what you consume and opting for minimal packaging.

Practical household examples:
  • Buy dry groceries, grains, and pulses in bulk rather than multiple small plastic packets.
  • Cook planned portions to prevent perishable food waste in the kitchen.
Third Priority

3. Reuse (తిరిగి వాడండి)

Use items multiple times in their original form instead of discarding them after a single use.

Practical household examples:
  • Wash and reuse sturdy glass jars and plastic tubs for kitchen pantry storage.
  • Use worn cotton clothes and old bedsheets as household cleaning rags instead of disposable wipes.
Fourth Priority

4. Repurpose (కొత్త ప్రయోజనానికి వాడండి)

Find a new, creative function for an old object instead of throwing it into the waste stream.

Practical household examples:
  • Convert empty coconut shells, large tins, or plastic paint buckets into balcony plant pots.
  • Cut cardboard delivery cartons into drawer dividers or school craft organizers.
Fifth Priority

5. Recycle (రీసైకిల్)

Send clean, segregated recyclable materials to processing facilities so materials can be remanufactured.

Practical household examples:
  • Bundle clean, dry newspapers, cardboard, and textbooks for local paper recyclers.
  • Rinse metal cans and PET beverage bottles before putting them into the dry waste bin.

Residents should also check local municipal instructions.
