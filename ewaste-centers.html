How to Hand Over E-Waste Safely

  • Wipe personal data, reset devices, and remove accounts before disposal.
  • Remove loose batteries where detachable and keep them in a small non-metallic pouch.
  • Keep electronics dry and unbroken. Do not smash screens or crush batteries.
  • Ask the authorised centre for an acknowledgement receipt or handover slip.
  • Never burn, melt, or dismantle e-waste at home. It releases toxic fumes and heavy metals.

Residents should also check local municipal instructions.
