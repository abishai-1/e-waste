The Four-Bin Framework

Green • Wet Waste

Food scraps, fruit and vegetable peels, tea leaves, and garden waste. Kept separate for composting or organic processing.

Read bin guide
Blue • Dry Waste

Clean paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass, and recyclable packaging. Keep clean and dry as practical.

Read bin guide
Red • Hazardous Waste

Chemicals, certain batteries, and bulbs. Need careful handling and appropriate collection.

Read bin guide
Purple • E-Waste

Old phones, chargers, cables, keyboards, and other electronics. Go to authorised e-waste collection channels.

Why This Project

Mixed waste makes recycling and composting harder, hazardous items can contaminate other materials, and reusable resources end up in dumpsites.

Confusion at source

Households often hesitate when sorting everyday packaging, soiled paper, or small electronic cables.

Mixed collection risks

When batteries or glass break in organic waste, wet waste cannot be converted into clean agricultural compost.

Local community impact

Segregating waste at home protects sanitation workers from injuries and keeps neighbourhood collection points cleaner.
